WELCOME

Our hotel sits in the Baia di San Gemiliano, a place full of history and calm.

Right next to the bay stands the Torre di San Gemiliano, built in the 17th century. Under the water, the sea floor is waiting for you to dive in and see how alive it is. The beaches have golden sand and water that goes from turquoise to emerald, and just by looking at them you start to slow down.

A few kilometres away is Tortolì, where history is everywhere. You can walk through Ortali 'e su Monti and its giants' tombs, visit the San Salvatore Natural Park, and see how people here have always cared for their dead. The lagoon is home to protected birds, and in the Church of Sant'Andrea you'll stop for a moment in front of its beautiful marble altar.
